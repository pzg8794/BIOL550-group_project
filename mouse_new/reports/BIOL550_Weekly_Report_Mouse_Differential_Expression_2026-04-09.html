Problems encountered

The main challenge this week was deciding how to interpret enrichment without forcing a misleading story. GO terms are highly redundant and often dominated by broad “regulation” labels, and splitting by direction also revealed an imbalance (many more upregulated than downregulated genes in the main bend-point core). This makes it easy to over-interpret generic pathway names or to imply symmetric biology when the data are not symmetric.

We have addressed some of this by keeping the PCA-first interpretation and bend-point–based narrowing, and by using direction-split enrichment as a diagnostic rather than a headline claim. This keeps the side-specific DRG contrasts central, retains geno_in_contra as supporting context, and treats weaker genotype/interaction branches as exploratory.
